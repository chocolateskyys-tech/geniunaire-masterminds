# Thread & E Signature Set

## Locked product structure

### ThreadFolio Glow
**Role:** Brain / tablet.

Captures, stores, scans, and organizes threads, files, ideas, decisions, skills, plans, products, services, personality, humor, quotes, memories, and unfinished work.

### E-Folio / E-Glow
**Role:** Portfolio / showpiece.

Turns approved ThreadFolio material into polished portfolios, business profiles, resumes, service menus, product catalogs, creator pages, business plans, proof-of-work pages, and GM-ready account profiles.

### E-Brochure / E-Map
**Role:** GM guide.

E-Brochure and E-Map are the same product. It works as the interactive brochure, park map, room guide, business-placement guide, account guide, and live navigation layer for Geniunaire MasterMinds.

## Official flow

**ThreadFolio remembers and understands it.**

**E-Folio builds and presents it.**

**E-Brochure/E-Map places, connects, and guides it.**

**GM gives approved results a place to operate, promote, sell, cast, produce, and grow.**

All three products are owned by LifeScope, but LifeScope is the parent company, not the primary consumer-facing brand.

## Auto-generation flow

1. Import a thread, file, note, project, or conversation into ThreadFolio.
2. Scan and categorize ideas, decisions, products, services, skills, people, quotes, tasks, deadlines, and opportunities.
3. Detect possible businesses, jobs, niches, creator lanes, offers, production concepts, and GM account types.
4. Require user approval before moving material forward.
5. Generate a polished E-Folio showpiece.
6. Assign the correct GM destination through E-Brochure/E-Map.
7. Prepare the matching GM account, storefront, room, channel, or business lane.
8. Require approval before publishing, charging money, or granting sensitive permissions.
